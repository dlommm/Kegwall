Rules every style follows

  • Starts only after the pour passes the wall's trigger (2 oz by default), so foam blips never flash the screen.
  • Shows measured ounces; the glass is scaled to the serving size in Settings.
  • Finishes on the recorded volume, then returns to the wall within 6 seconds.
