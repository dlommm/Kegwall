# Cleaning reminders and unclaimed pours

## Line-cleaning reminders

The dashboard and **Taps & kegs** page show cleaning reminders for taps with an active keg
containing beer. Empty, kicked, and archived taps do not create cleaning reminders. Cleaning
history is retained when a keg becomes empty; putting a new keg on the tap makes it eligible for
reminders again.

To pause reminders for a particular tap, open **Taps & kegs**, expand that tap's details, and
choose **Ignore reminders**. Choose **Resume reminders** to turn them back on. Ignoring reminders
does not record a cleaning or change the cleaning history. Dashboard cleaning actions open the
matching tap so its details are easy to find.

## Unclaimed pours

The **Pours** page lists recent unclaimed pours and refreshes periodically. Use **Refresh** to
check immediately, or assign a pour to a drinker to remove it from the unclaimed list. Older pours
remain available to administrators for assignment.

The public menu only offers claims during its recent-pour window (10 minutes). A pour that has
aged out of that window can still be assigned from the admin Pours page.
